# TS-R4-08 — Post-entry bracket verification

**Status:** done  
**Priority:** P1  
**Issue:** [#86](https://github.com/GlitchTrader/glitch-topstep/issues/86)  
**Gateway version target:** 0.1.5+  
**Depends on:** TS-BRACKET-01  
**Profile consumers:** `glitch-topstep-hermes-profile:GTHP-020`

## Objective

After `ENTER_*` fill, verify SL/TP protection landed on venue and expose terminal `protection_status` in packet.

## Planned behavior

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `pending` | Fill acknowledged; bracket not yet confirmed. |
| `confirmed` | SL/TP verified on venue within timeout. |
| `failed` | Timeout or venue mismatch; operator alert path. |
| `unknown` | Reconciliation incomplete. |

## Verification timeout

`BRACKET_VERIFICATION_TIMEOUT_MS` is **30 seconds** from first observed fill (`fill_observed_utc` on the entry receipt). The coordinator reconciles on every venue refresh; packet `protection.protection_status` and ledger events (`bracket_verification_confirmed` / `bracket_verification_failed`) reflect transitions. No auto-flatten on `pending` or `failed`.

## Operator retry on `failed`

1. Confirm Auto OCO Brackets are enabled (see `docs/OPERATIONS.md` epoch checklist).
2. Inspect `/ownership` and TopstepX working orders for missing SL/TP children.
3. Hermes may issue `EXIT` to flatten; gateway does not auto-flatten without operator policy.
4. If brackets were lost after partial exit, the coordinator may re-arm on the next reconcile when quote is available (`tranche_protection_rearmed` event).

## Acceptance

- Coordinator polls/reconciles after entry within documented timeout.
- Packet and events reflect state transitions.
- Regression tests; operator retry documented.
- Hermes may plan `EXIT` on `failed`; no auto-flatten without operator policy.

## Stop line

Factual protection verification only—no hidden strategy on `pending`.
